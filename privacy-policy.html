Privacy Policy for VehicleEx Pro

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Summary: VehicleEx Pro is an offline-first application. All your vehicle numbers, document expiration dates, photos, and records are stored exclusively on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with external servers.

1. Data Collection and Storage

VehicleEx Pro does not collect personal data. All information you enter (including vehicle registration details and uploaded document photos) is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB / browser local storage. No data is sent to external servers or third-party analytics services.

2. Camera and Storage Permissions

The application requires permission to access your device camera and local file storage solely to allow you to take photos of your vehicle documents or upload existing document files. These images remain stored locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

VehicleEx Pro does not integrate third-party tracking, advertising, or data collection SDKs.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us via our official repository or developer contact provided on the store listing.
